# Kitten Nest construction log · 2026-08-13

## Scope

Replace the isolated Nestward behavior prototype with the first formal, immersive 2.5D world release, then turn the first phone review into a reusable interaction vertical slice. The prototype was treated as evidence of intent only; its HUD, placeholder geometry, temporary room coordinates, screen-fixed bubble, hard-coded action positions, and per-frame random scenery were not retained as product rules.

Production `/cloud` received only one transparent Pebble Jar entry and a validated same-origin route action. No production scene owner, navigation stack, text content, Supabase value, schema, RLS policy, bucket, Storage object, or MCP target changed.

## Formal world rebuild

The release introduces two `1536 × 1024` authored reference worlds:

```text
indoor:  bedroom, wardrobe, window, sofa, desk, message wall, tea area, garden door
outdoor: cottage threshold, mailbox, bench, swing, flower bed, tea table,
         wishing fountain, firefly pond, vine bower, distant sunset valley
```

The indoor plate established the accepted warm, detailed illustrated direction. The outdoor plate remains a functional spatial reference and is explicitly not the final art direction: its visibly pixel-game rendering must be replaced by the indoor world's finer illustration language when the garden is rebuilt in layers.

The formal model owns visual hit regions, walk blockers, approach sockets, named multi-actor slots, object mounts, and foreground occlusion layers. The runtime asks that model where an actor belongs; no interaction controller owns a second set of room coordinates.

Camera dead-zone following, walk-depth projection, A* pathfinding, perspective scaling, actor depth sorting, foreground/background passage, object-bound actions, companion follow/carry behavior, shared seating, swing pushing, and indoor/outdoor continuity were rebuilt around the new art.

The visible demo title and permanent HUD were removed. A faint safe-area return control remains. Floor taps walk; object taps first approach the object's authored socket; direct doors transition without a redundant menu; multi-action objects show a short local context menu.

## Phone-review interaction vertical slice

The first installed-phone review correctly exposed that rotating or translating a standing sprite is not an interaction system. Bed actions had placed Kitten on the floor, indoor people were too small, bubbles could cover a speaker's face, the Moonlight Wings were attached near the knees, carried Naili could not be put down, and the camera could only move when Kitten walked.

The corrective slice adds:

- refined transparent idle and four-frame walk artwork for Kitten and Hubby, plus a refined Naili sprite;
- one normalized facing contract: Hubby's left-facing source frames mirror as a whole when he walks right, including his head;
- authored bed sit, lie, and lean poses instead of a rotated standing figure;
- bed-owned mounts that define actor position, visual baseline, pose, size, and facing;
- an exact-pixel foreground mask redrawn from the original room plate so the mattress edge correctly covers mounted actors;
- separate indoor and outdoor actor-height profiles;
- bubble anchors calculated from the currently rendered pose bounds, above the speaker's head;
- single-finger camera panning, two-finger zooming, and camera follow that resumes only after an intentional floor walk;
- Moonlight Wings attached to the upper back with deterministic flutter and light motes;
- an explicit close control on local action menus;
- carried-Naili hit testing and a working put-down action;
- a stateful garden gate whose closed state must be opened before its future exit can be used;
- an opt-in `cgPortal` long-press contract. No current object is assigned a CG portal, so ordinary objects do not acquire invisible long-press destinations.

The original Pebble Jar remains the only Cat Nest entry. No second home hotspot was added.

The shared PWA manifest now scopes the installed app to `/`, so `/cloud` and `/v2/nestward/` belong to one standalone application instead of Nestward being treated as an out-of-scope browser page. Both entry documents use the same manifest and app icon.

## Wishing fountain and wings

The outdoor fountain owns two interactions: make a wish and listen. The first wish unlocks the Moonlight Wings, equips them immediately, records the unlock on the device, and makes movement visibly lift Kitten above her ground shadow. The indoor wardrobe can store or re-equip the wings on later visits.

Three old outfit buttons per person were removed from the formal release because the new sprite artwork did not yet have matching visual variants. The wardrobe now describes the actually rendered outfits and exposes only the wing control that produces finished visual behavior.

The initial fountain overlay used broad pale water arcs that competed with the painted fountain. It was replaced with small, low-opacity droplets bound to the water column plus warm wish motes.

## Phone-review closeout: body zones, speech, carry, and layered prop

The second installed-phone review defined a deliberately bounded closeout for
the first edition. The full room was not redrawn and production `/cloud` was not
opened. The accepted fixes are reusable runtime capabilities:

- indoor standing Kitten and Hubby are exactly 35 percent larger (`190 → 257`,
  `218 → 294`); outdoor scale remains scene-authored;
- the bed lie pose keeps an authored `310` world-unit width instead of inheriting
  the standing-scale increase;
- clear-floor sit, lie, and stand actions reuse real pose artwork for both
  people;
- upper-body taps own speech and lower-body taps own actions; inspecting either
  zone does not eject a mounted actor;
- local menus choose a side, below, or above position that fits the viewport
  instead of covering the actor by default;
- bubbles and menus use larger phone-readable type, and Kitten speech uses the
  shared peach-pink author token;
- bubbles are themselves invisible close/pause targets; character taps resume
  or advance the stored cursor;
- the installed shell and Canvas size from their actual rendered container,
  including `visualViewport` and `ResizeObserver` changes, rather than trusting
  a stale `innerHeight` that can expose a bottom strip;
- the swing uses one authored mount centered on its painted seat, and the old
  construction-looking gate overlay no longer paints over the garden;
- ordinary walking uses the complete authored four-frame stride and mirrors the
  entire source figure; no procedural body bob is added on top of those frames;
- princess carry is a clean two-frame combined animation with the same complete-
  figure direction contract and Hubby-owned path/camera movement;
- carry owns a nine-turn automatic roadside conversation, while each person also
  owns a longer natural ambient script for manual or hybrid discovery;
- a separate moss-green reading chair proves the formal three-pass prop contract:
  chair back, mounted actor, then only the two front arms. The former broad seat
  mask was removed because it amputated the sitter's authored hanging legs.

The new carry and chair artwork was produced as isolated assets, normalized to
true alpha, and committed under `v2/nestward/assets/`. They do not depend on a
remote image service at runtime.

Deferred to the formal second-edition art build: mint hair correction, replacing
all remaining baked furniture with separable assets, redrawing the garden in the
indoor illustration language, preventing mirrored typography through directional
art variants, and authoring bespoke composite poses for every complex object.

## Home entry and safety

`home.nestwardEnterHot` is base-image-locked to the Pebble Jar and dispatches:

```json
{ "type": "route.open", "target": "/v2/nestward/" }
```

Manifest validation rejects non-absolute, protocol-relative, or backslash-containing route targets. Runtime dispatch resolves the route against the current origin and rejects an origin change before navigation. Nestward itself stays isolated at `/v2/nestward/` and returns to `/cloud` through its own quiet edge control.

Production launch review then exposed one ordering race that static viewport
checks could not reproduce: `ResizeObserver` may fire as soon as the shell has
layout, before the two world plates finish decoding. The observer is still

without adding a timeout, retry loop, or duplicate image request.

## Carry and touch-state regression closeout

A later installed-phone traversal exposed seven symptoms with three shared root
causes: the shortened walk loop skipped the authored transitional phases, carry
was cleared by both scene and object-arrival callbacks, and a newly mounted
action menu could receive the synthetic click belonging to the Canvas gesture
that opened it. Oversized rectangular garden hits also consumed visible path.

The closeout restores the complete four-frame walk cycle for both people and
keeps the authored torso weight shift free of an additional procedural bob. The
princess-carry renderer locks the embrace, heads, and shoulders to one base
frame and replaces only Hubby's lower stride region, so walking legs no longer
make both bodies lurch. Carry now survives indoor/outdoor transitions and object
inspection. Only `放小猫下来`, or choosing a real object action after arrival,
puts Kitten down; closing or merely passing a menu does not.

Context menus now require a pointerdown that began inside the menu before they
accept a pointer-generated click. Reclining body zones follow the accepted
rotated rule (head/upper torso for actions, legs for speech). The reading chair's
broad center foreground mask was deleted, the swing mount moved ten world units
up, and swing/garden/tea/gate hits became visible-shape polygons. Their collision
blocks were narrowed with the same intent, restoring the central garden path as
walkable floor rather than an invisible object field.
