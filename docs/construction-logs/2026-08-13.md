# Kitten Nest construction log · 2026-08-13

## Scope

Replace the isolated Nestward behavior prototype with the first formal, immersive 2.5D world release. The prototype was treated as evidence of intent only; its HUD, placeholder geometry, temporary room coordinates, screen-fixed bubble, hard-coded action positions, and per-frame random scenery were not retained as product rules.

Production `/cloud` received only one transparent Pebble Jar entry and a validated same-origin route action. No production scene owner, navigation stack, text content, Supabase value, schema, RLS policy, bucket, Storage object, or MCP target changed.

## Formal world rebuild

The release introduces two complete `1536 × 1024` authored worlds:

```text
indoor:  bedroom, wardrobe, window, sofa, desk, message wall, tea area, garden door
outdoor: cottage threshold, mailbox, bench, swing, flower bed, tea table,
         wishing fountain, firefly pond, vine bower, distant sunset valley
```

Both use detailed cozy pixel-art plates and separate transparent sprites for Kitten, Hubby, and Naili. The formal model owns visual hit regions, walk blockers, approach sockets, and named multi-actor slots. The runtime asks that model where an actor belongs; no interaction controller owns a second set of room coordinates.

Camera dead-zone following, walk-depth projection, A* pathfinding, perspective scaling, actor depth sorting, foreground/background passage, object-bound actions, companion follow/carry behavior, shared seating, swing pushing, and indoor/outdoor continuity were rebuilt around the new art.

The visible demo title and permanent HUD were removed. A faint safe-area return control remains. Floor taps walk; object taps first approach the object's authored socket; direct doors transition without a redundant menu; multi-action objects show a short local context menu.

## Wishing fountain and wings

The outdoor fountain owns two interactions: make a wish and listen. The first wish unlocks the Moonlight Wings, equips them immediately, records the unlock on the device, and makes movement visibly lift Kitten above her ground shadow. The indoor wardrobe can store or re-equip the wings on later visits.

Three old outfit buttons per person were removed from the formal release because the new sprite artwork did not yet have matching visual variants. The wardrobe now describes the actually rendered outfits and exposes only the wing control that produces finished visual behavior.

The initial fountain overlay used broad pale water arcs that competed with the painted fountain. It was replaced with small, low-opacity droplets bound to the water column plus warm wish motes.

## Home entry and safety

`home.nestwardEnterHot` is base-image-locked to the Pebble Jar and dispatches:

```json
{ "type": "route.open", "target": "/v2/nestward/" }
```

Manifest validation rejects non-absolute, protocol-relative, or backslash-containing route targets. Runtime dispatch resolves the route against the current origin and rejects an origin change before navigation. Nestward itself stays isolated at `/v2/nestward/` and returns to `/cloud` through its own quiet edge control.

## QA and corrections

The first mobile traversal exposed an indoor camera boundary that stopped 76 world units before the new image ended. The boundary was corrected to the full formal world width, making the garden door completely visible and tappable. Two sofa slots and one bench slot were also moved away from the exact edge of adjacent collision padding so their authored destinations remain exact.
