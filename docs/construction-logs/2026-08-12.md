## Dialogue and standalone TextPort rules

All three `seasideWalk` dialogue groups now declare `camera.policy: manual`. TextPortController does not add grouped ports to the panorama reveal queue, so first reveal, same-speaker continuation, speaker switch, copy-height change, Layout completion, and scene re-entry leave `scrollLeft` unchanged. Edge-growth coordinates remain unchanged: handhold grows upward from its protected lower edge; bracelet and stall grow downward from their protected upper edges.

Standalone/ambient ports use an in-memory map keyed by object id and a deterministic queue fingerprint. The saved record contains line index, visibility, and `hasShown`. A closed line returns closed after scene navigation; the next tap advances exactly once. New published content invalidates the old record. Controller destruction clears the map, so completely closing/reopening the PWA resets the visit. Only `coffeeCorner.bubble` and `lapClose.bubble` explicitly opt into initial visibility; ordinary ports default closed. No unread indicator was introduced.

## Final production acceptance: six durable rules

The morning implementation and its iPhone follow-up are closed as six product rules rather than a loose list of visual patches:

1. **Manual panorama camera.** Opening or advancing a beach dialogue never moves the horizontal camera. The visitor owns `scrollLeft`; bubbles remain locked to authored image coordinates and grow only away from protected face boundaries.
2. **Session-only standalone continuity.** An ambient bubble resumes the exact line and open/closed state after scene navigation inside one app visit. A full PWA restart resets it. Ordinary ambient bubbles begin closed; the two established greeters remain explicit exceptions. No unread marker is introduced.
3. **Explicit authorship.** Browser and notebook writes are Vicky; MCP writes are Alex. Vicky ink is the shared pink token and Alex ink is the shared dark token across bubbles and notebook pages. The one guarded legacy classification assigned all 19 known pages without changing their copy or timestamps.
4. **Weather is text on glassless air.** The registered temperature and description keep the accepted translucent day/night colors and 4.8-second vertical float. The daytime-only trapezoid pseudo-element is removed; no background plate is allowed behind the window reading.
5. **Pebble jar owns its magic.** Thirty-two manifest-owned particles drift upward inside the accepted jar box, independently of room-wide sparkles.
6. **Day/Night is a retained-image handoff.** The room art crossfades for 520 ms while text, hotspots, effects, and panels remain mounted. The outgoing image stays composited until its handoff fade finishes, and the fixed stage shading remains above both images, preventing the final iOS WebKit black-band flash.
