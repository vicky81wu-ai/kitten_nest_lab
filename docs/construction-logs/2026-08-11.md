# Kitten Nest construction log · 2026-08-11

## Scope

Remove horizontal camera yo-yo when Alex and Vicky alternate dialogue in the three existing beach panoramas. Preserve the accepted image-world rule: bubbles and hotspots stay pinned to the base image, while only future rescue navigation may be viewport-fixed.

## Decision

One conversation is one camera unit. A new top-level `dialogueGroups` registry owns:

```text
scene owner
TextPort members
camera policy: groupLock
normalized base-image focusX
```

The artwork inspection showed that Vicky is foreground/POV in these frames rather than a second distant left-side subject. The previous `.36` Vicky bubble anchors therefore created false spatial separation of roughly one phone width. The selected group foci are `.51` for handhold, `.48` for bracelet, and `.56` for stall order. Both speakers in each scene now share that X while retaining separate vertical anchors.

## Runtime behavior

The first revealed member after scene entry waits for `layout:ready`, then centers the group focus once. The group remains focused for that scene entry, so later speaker switches and copy-width changes never invoke per-bubble reveal correction. Manual horizontal drag remains authoritative. Leaving and re-entering the scene resets the one-focus lifecycle.

Ungrouped panorama TextPorts keep the existing measured safe-edge reveal fallback. No route, scene stack, target id, queue, state field, MCP surface, Supabase object, or Storage asset changed.
